Welcome to George Mason's Computer Science Department!

The Department of Computer Science (CS) combines academic excellence with real-world relevance. As one of the largest and fastest-growing computer science departments in Virginia, we are committed to preparing students for leadership in a digital-first world.
The Department of Computer Science offers academic pathways that provide the flexibility and depth needed to thrive in today's technology landscape. The ABET-accredited undergraduate programs build a strong foundation in computing with concentrations like Artificial Intelligence (AI), Computer Game Design, Linguistics, Bioinformatics, and Technology Policy.
